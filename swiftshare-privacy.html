2. Architecture & Data Philosophy

SwiftShare was designed from the ground up to empower users to communicate and share data freely without relying on cloud infrastructure:

  • Peer-to-Peer Transmission: Transfers and communication operate directly between devices over local sockets (TCP/UDP) or Wi-Fi Direct/Hotspot.
  • Zero Cloud Intermediaries: We do not route your files or messages through any cloud relay, remote server, or intermediate database.
  • Local Encryption: Chat messages and signaling between peers are encrypted end-to-end locally using military-grade AES-256 encryption.

3. Information We Collect & Process

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Profile Display Name & Avatar: When you configure your profile, your chosen display name and avatar image are stored locally on your device's private storage (Room database). When your device is discoverable on a local network, your name and avatar are broadcast strictly to local peers via mDNS/UDP so they can recognize your device. This information is never uploaded to any external server.
  • Shared Files & Media: Any photos, videos, music, documents, or apps you select for transfer remain on your device until sent directly to your chosen peer. SwiftShare never uploads, scans, or analyzes your files.
  • Chat Messages: Text messages exchanged within direct or group chats are stored solely in your local device database. You can clear chat history or delete messages at any time directly from the app.

B. Information Automatically Processed Locally

  • Local Network IP Addresses: To establish direct socket connections, SwiftShare identifies your device's assigned local IP address (e.g., 192.168.x.x). This address is used solely for local socket communication and is never transmitted over the internet to us.
  • Device Model & Type: Your device model (e.g., Samsung, Pixel, PC) may be shared locally with peers to display a descriptive icon in the nearby devices radar.

6. Data Retention & User Rights (Data Deletion)

  • No Cloud Retention: Because we do not upload your data to cloud servers, DUNIA Studio does not hold, store, or retain any copies of your files, chat transcripts, or media.
  • Complete Local Control: All data resides exclusively on your device. You have full control to delete chat messages, remove group memberships, clear transfer history, or delete received files from your local storage at any time.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the Application deletes all locally cached databases, app preferences, and temporary files immediately.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SwiftShare is a general-utility productivity tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under 13. 8. Security Safeguards

We take data security seriously:

  • Chat messages transmitted between peer devices over local networks are encrypted using AES-256.
  • Direct Web Share operates over an isolated local HTTP server with customizable session PIN authentication to prevent unauthorized local network access.
  • Hotspot sessions generate randomized WPA2/WPA3 passphrases.
